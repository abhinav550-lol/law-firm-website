
### Required Mock Data

* **Lawyers:** 2–4 lawyer profiles

  * Name
  * Photo
  * Qualification
  * Designation
  * Enrolment number
  * Bar Council
  * Areas of practice
  * Short bio

* **Practice Areas / Services:** 5–8 services

  * Name
  * Short description
  * Detailed description
  * Relevant icon/image

* **Blog / Legal Resources:** 5–10 articles

  * Title
  * Cover image
  * Author
  * Category
  * Short excerpt
  * Content
  * Published date

* **Contact / Firm:** 1–2 office locations

  * Firm name
  * Address
  * Phone
  * Email
  * Office hours
  * Google Maps location

* **Contact Form:** Sample submissions

  * Name
  * Phone
  * Email
  * Subject
  * Message

* **FAQs:** 5–10 common legal questions + answers

* **Firm Information:**

  * Firm name/logo
  * Short introduction
  * About/history
  * Professional approach
  * Disclaimer text
