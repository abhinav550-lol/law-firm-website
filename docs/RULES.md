# Legal Website Rules — India

Rules for building and editing this law firm website.

## 1. No Advertising / Solicitation

* Do not create content that promotes or solicits legal services.
* Avoid phrases like **"best lawyer"**, **"top law firm"**, **"guaranteed results"**, **"hire us"**, **"free consultation"**, etc.
* Do not create SEO pages intended primarily to attract clients.

## 2. No Success Claims

* Do not display success rates, case-winning statistics, client counts, testimonials, ratings, or claims of favourable outcomes.
* Do not publish "successful cases" as promotional material.

## 3. Professional Information Only

Content should primarily provide factual professional information:

* Advocate names
* Qualifications
* Enrolment numbers
* State Bar Council
* Bar Association membership
* Office address
* Phone/email
* Areas of practice

## 4. No Aggressive CTAs

Avoid:

* "Book Now"
* "Call Now"
* "Get Legal Help"
* "Hire Our Lawyers"
* "Contact Us for Your Case"

Use neutral wording such as **"Contact Information"**.

## 5. Privacy & Confidentiality

* Do not request unnecessary case details or sensitive personal information.
* Never imply that submitting a website form creates an advocate-client relationship.
* Use HTTPS and provide appropriate privacy/disclaimer pages.

## 6. Content

* Legal articles must be informational, not promotional.
* Do not provide case-specific legal advice through website content.
* Avoid language guaranteeing or predicting legal outcomes.

## 7. Compliance First

* Follow the **Bar Council of India Rules**, especially **Rule 36** regarding advertising and solicitation.
* When uncertain whether content is promotional, prefer the conservative/informational approach.
* Final legal/compliance approval should come from the advocates before publication.

**Core principle:** This website is an **informational professional profile**, not a marketing or client-acquisition website.
